Questions

I lost my 12 words. Can you get them back?

No. Nobody can, not even us: the words never leave your device. If your vault is still open on your iPhone, use Export vault in the Lists menu now and keep the file. You can import it into a new vault later.

How do I open my vault on another iPhone or on the website?

Type the same 12 words. They open the same vault everywhere.

How do I share a vault?

In the Lists menu, tap Share (read-only). warket copies a share key. Send it to the other person. On an iPhone they tap Open a shared vault and paste it; on the website they paste it where you would type the words. They can look but never change anything.

How do I stop sharing?

A share key keeps working until the vault is deleted, and the same 12 words always give the same share key. To cut someone off: Export vault, Delete vault, then make a new vault with new words and import the file.

How do I back up or move my data?

Export vault saves a file with your lists and assets. Import vault adds a file's lists to the open vault. Lists with the same name are merged.

How do I delete my vault?

In the Lists menu, tap Delete vault. It asks twice, then erases everything in the vault from the server. It can't be undone.

Is warket a crypto wallet?

No. warket holds no money or coins, connects to no blockchain and has no wallet keys. The 12 words only open your notes.

Does warket show prices or give advice?

No. warket is a notebook for the assets you follow. It doesn't show prices, trade or give financial advice.

What does warket keep about me?

No name, no email, no account. Read the privacy policy for exactly what is stored and where.
